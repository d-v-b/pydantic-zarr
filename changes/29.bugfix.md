Writing and reading a hierarchy now keeps the fields that a subclass adds to a node, in `pydantic_zarr.v2` and `pydantic_zarr.v3`. `from_stored_metadata_json` and `from_flat` now validate the hierarchy's whole document as the class, so each member is validated by its own class, in its own scope. In v3, `to_flat` and `own_metadata` keep a group's added fields in the `BaseGroupSpec`'s `extra_fields`, so `to_stored_metadata_json` writes them for every member. A group's `model_dump` now dumps each member by its own class, whatever the members type, so `replace` keeps a member's added fields and `diff` reports changes to them. A spec now reads its own dump back equal when its class has a computed field or a field with an alias. Previously, in v3, a group member's added field was dropped from its stored document and read back as the default, and a field that only a member's scope claims made `from_stored_metadata_json` raise. In v2 and v3, `from_flat` dropped added fields, `replace` dropped a member's added fields, and `diff` did not report them. A computed field or an aliased field was read back into `extra_fields`.
