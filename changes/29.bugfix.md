Writing and reading a hierarchy now keeps the fields that a subclass adds to a node, in `pydantic_zarr.v2` and `pydantic_zarr.v3`. `to_stored_metadata_json` writes them for every member, and `from_stored_metadata_json` and `from_flat` read them back. In v3, `to_flat` and `own_metadata` keep a group's added fields in the `BaseGroupSpec`'s `extra_fields`. Previously, a group member's added field was dropped from its stored document and read back as the default. A root's or an array's added field made `from_stored_metadata_json` raise, and `from_flat(to_flat(g))` dropped it.
