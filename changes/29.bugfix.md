Writing and reading a hierarchy now keeps the fields that a subclass adds to a node, in `pydantic_zarr.v2` and `pydantic_zarr.v3`. `to_stored_metadata_json` writes them for every member, and `from_stored_metadata_json` and `from_flat` read them back. These methods now validate the whole hierarchy's document as the class, so each member is validated by its own class, in its own scope. In v3, `to_flat` and `own_metadata` keep a group's added fields in the `BaseGroupSpec`'s `extra_fields`. A group's `model_dump`, and so `replace` and `diff`, now dump each member by its own class. Previously, a group member's added field was dropped from its stored document and read back as the default. `from_flat` dropped added fields, `replace` dropped a member's added fields, and `diff` did not report them. A field that only a member's scope claims made `from_stored_metadata_json` raise.
